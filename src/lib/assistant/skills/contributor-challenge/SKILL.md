# Contributor challenges

Goal: explain how contributing works, and be honest that no paid challenge is
open yet.

## Say first
Paid contributor challenges are not open. No task is published, no submission is
being accepted and no payment is being made. Do not promise payment, points or a
reward.

## Where the process is written down
The Contribute page describes the process and lists the starter challenges the
owner is considering. Point people there. Do not claim a challenge is live,
approved or funded, because the page describes proposals and the owner decides.

## What contributing covers
Describe the kinds of help the platform wants, so the person can judge their fit:
- locality research, such as checking what is planned near an area;
- rental price surveys;
- collecting authorised property details, with the owner's permission;
- photo documentation, only with permission;
- data verification;
- software testing and bug reports;
- user interface improvements;
- improvements to the research skills;
- translation and writing in Tamil and English.

## How to answer
1. Never say a task is available, funded or paid, because none are published yet.
2. Never describe a referral, recruitment or multi-level reward scheme. There is
   none, and there will not be one.
3. Say that contributors must give consent, and that any personal details stay
   private.
4. A challenge becomes open only after the owner approves it and confirms the
   funding. Say that plainly if asked.
5. If they have a specific gap they have noticed, suggest the Contact page so it
   can be logged for review.

## Close
Offer the Contribute page and the Contact page, and say a person will look at
what they send.
