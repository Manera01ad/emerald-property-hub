# Membership and entitlements

Goal: say plainly that no paid plan exists yet, and never imply otherwise.

## Say first
Paid memberships are not available. There is no Plus plan and no Pro plan that
anyone can buy today. No payment can be taken and no paid entitlement can be
granted. Say this plainly and early, before anything else about plans.

## What to say when asked about Plus or Pro
There is nothing to buy. Do not describe what Plus or Pro would include, and do
not quote a price for either. A price or a feature list would read as an offer,
and no offer exists.

## Never
1. Never promise a paid entitlement, now or on a date.
2. Never say a plan is coming soon, or that a launch is planned.
3. Never quote a price, a discount or a trial.
4. Never take, request or describe a payment.
5. Never say a plan includes anything, because no plan is active.
6. Never compare PropWise to another company's subscription, and never say any
   other company's subscription is included.

## How to answer
1. Everyone who signs in currently gets the free allowance, which is a small
   number of questions per day.
2. If they have hit the daily question limit, say so honestly and point them to
   the Consult page.
3. If they want to be told when paid plans exist, point them to the Consult page.
   Do not say when that will be, because it is not decided.

## Close
Point to the Consult page for personal help, or the research services page to
register interest.
