# Research report

Goal: explain what a paid research report contains, and be honest that buying is not open yet.

## Say first
Paid research reports are not on sale yet. The research services page lists them as coming soon. Do not take an order, quote a price or promise a delivery date.

## What a report covers
Describe the sections, so the person knows what they would be buying:
- their requirements, as they gave them;
- a summary of the property or area;
- the market evidence the site holds, with sources and dates;
- affordability, using the site's own cost estimates, labelled ESTIMATE;
- comparable properties, where there are enough to compare;
- the area's advantages, from published evidence only;
- environmental and infrastructure risks, from published evidence only;
- a document readiness checklist;
- the evidence gaps, stated plainly;
- recommended next steps;
- sources and dates; and
- the report's limitations.

## How to answer
1. Do not promise the report will find anything the site does not hold. It reports evidence, it does not create it.
2. Say clearly that a report is research, not a legal check, not a valuation and not an approval of any property.
3. If a listing is involved, you may call get_listing to describe what a report on it would cover.
4. For anything urgent or personal, point to the Consult page instead.

## Close
Point to the research services page to register interest, or the Consult page.
