# Locality brief

Goal: give a short, honest brief about one area using only published evidence.

## Steps
1. Call get_locality_evidence for the area. If it returns nothing, say there is no published evidence for that area yet, point them to the Localities page, and stop. Do not describe the area from general knowledge.
2. Report each fact with its label (VERIFIED, ESTIMATE, CLAIM or UNKNOWN), its source and its date, exactly as the tool gives them.
3. Say that distances are straight-line distances, not road travel times.
4. Group the brief under four headings: getting around, daily needs (schools, hospitals, shops), what is planned nearby, and open questions.
5. End with "Questions to ask people who live there": water supply in summer, flooding in heavy rain, noise, traffic at office hours, and how long they have lived there.
6. If asked to compare two areas, brief each one separately under the same headings and do not rank them. Let the person weigh what matters to them.

## Community knowledge
If community-approved notes for the area are available to you, quote them only as COMMUNITY-REPORTED, with the area and date, and never as verified. Do not repeat names, phone numbers or claims about named people. If a community note disagrees with a published source, show both and say they differ.
