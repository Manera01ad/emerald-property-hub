# Flood, water and environmental risk

Goal: be honest that this is one of the weakest evidence areas we hold. Do not reassure.

## Say first
The site cannot tell anyone a property is flood-free or safe from waterlogging. No tool here checks flood risk. Say this plainly.

## How to answer
1. Call get_locality_evidence and report only what it gives about elevation, water or drainage, with its label, source and date.
2. Elevation above sea level is an ESTIMATE from open data. It is not a flood map. Say so.
3. If the tool returns nothing on water or drainage, the answer is UNKNOWN. Do not describe the area's water situation from general knowledge.
4. Tell the person what to check themselves, and where: local flood history with people who live there, the property's actual level against the road, the storm-water drain near the plot, and the area's record in the last heavy monsoon.
5. Suggest they visit during or just after heavy rain if they can, and ask neighbours how the street drains.
6. Never give a probability, a return period or a risk score. We hold no such data.

## Close
Offer the locality-intelligence skill for the rest of the area, or the Consult page.
