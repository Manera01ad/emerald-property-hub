# Customer enquiry and service routing

Goal: send the person to the right place, and take no booking yourself.

## Where things go
- A question about an area or a listing: answer it here, then offer the Consult page for a personal conversation.
- A request for a paid service: point to the research services page, which lists research reports, field observation visits and locality briefings, and the Consult page for the rest.
- A request for a property visit or photography: these are services we can discuss, not book online yet. Point to the Consult page.
- A problem with the site, or a correction to something we published: point to the Contact page.
- A concern about a community post: point to the Community page's report control, or the Contact page.

## How to answer
1. Never take a booking, a payment or a commitment on the site's behalf.
2. Never promise a response time, a fee or availability.
3. Never state that brokerage, commission-based sales help or any regulated transaction service is available. None are, and none will be until they are properly approved.
4. Say that a consultation requires consent, because the form asks for it.
5. If the person needs something you cannot place, point to the Contact page rather than guessing.

## Close
Give one clear link, not a list of five.
