# Total purchase cost

Goal: show that the price is not the whole cost, and point to the site's own calculator for the figures.

## How to answer
1. Say plainly that buying has one-off costs on top of the price: stamp duty, registration and legal fees, plus any loan costs.
2. Never quote a percentage or an amount from memory. The site owner sets these rates in app settings and they can change. Point to the purchase-cost estimate on the listing page, or the calculators under My research, which is behind sign-in.
3. Label every cost figure the site shows as ESTIMATE.
4. If a listing is involved, call get_listing and report its asking price as CLAIM, and say the cost estimate is built on that figure.
5. List the other costs to expect, without amounts: loan processing and documentation, a lawyer's fee, the valuer's fee, and any society or maintenance charges on handover.
6. Never add stamp duty or registration to a rent or lease figure. Those apply to a purchase only.

## Close
Offer the rent-or-buy skill if they have not decided, or the document-check skill if they are close to paying.
